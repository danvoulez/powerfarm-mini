# 1. Declaration of existence

Powerfarm exists to discover, measure, and document the best available ways to produce software using artificial intelligence and contemporary computing systems.

Powerfarm does not exist to invent models, agents, frameworks, protocols, databases, or infrastructure as ends in themselves. It exists to learn how to use, combine, evaluate, and replace what the wider industry makes possible.

> **Founding thesis**
>
> External technological progress is Powerfarm's raw material. Accumulated operational knowledge is Powerfarm's primary product. Better technology outside Powerfarm should increase, not reduce, Powerfarm's opportunity to learn.

# 2. The structural change Powerfarm studies

Software production is moving from a regime dominated by humans directly operating files, languages, IDEs, terminals, and version-control systems toward a regime in which intelligent systems increasingly interpret intent, gather context, plan, generate, modify, test, review, and operate software.

The central problem therefore expands from "how to write software" to "how to organize intelligence, context, tools, representation, verification, time, and cost so software is produced as well as currently possible."

# 3. Object of study

The meaningful unit is not a model or tool in isolation. It is the operational system used to produce a result. Depending on the question, that system may include models, providers, agents, tools, browsers, sandboxes, repositories, semantic representations, retrieval, memory, compilers, tests, hardware, APIs, humans, and verification mechanisms.

Powerfarm studies these systems at the level where a real decision can change.

# 4. The asset Powerfarm compounds

Powerfarm's primary long-term asset is a longitudinal body of evidence about how different configurations of intelligence produce outcomes under different conditions.

- Raw experimental evidence and real-world execution history.

- Versioned methods, benchmark history, and known measurement limitations.

- Claims with supporting and contradicting evidence, scope, confidence, and freshness.

- Decision models showing which configuration is preferred under which constraints.

- Historical knowledge of what changed, when it changed, and why a previous recommendation stopped being best.

Code, infrastructure, tools, hardware, and vendor access are important only insofar as they strengthen this compounding knowledge system or enable useful products from it.

# 5. Founding commitments

| **Commitment**                | **Meaning**                                                                                                                  |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Evidence primacy              | Material claims and recommendations ultimately trace to evidence, or are explicitly marked provisional.                      |
| Frontier positive             | Powerfarm welcomes superior external technology and tests whether it changes important decisions.                            |
| No technology attachment      | No model, provider, framework, benchmark, architecture, protocol, or implementation receives permanent status by preference. |
| Knowledge over tooling        | Internal technology is a means. Evidence, learning, and decision capability are the durable asset.                           |
| Reuse before invention        | Credible external alternatives are considered before proprietary equivalents are built.                                      |
| Decision relevance            | Important research should be able to state which decision its result could change.                                           |
| Explicit uncertainty          | Uncertainty is carried with the claim or recommendation instead of being hidden by prose, rankings, or scores.               |
| Negative evidence matters     | Null, negative, contradictory, and failed results remain part of institutional knowledge.                                    |
| No silent history rewrite     | Material past methods, beliefs, results, and recommendations remain historically addressable when superseded.                |
| Reality above benchmarks      | Benchmarks are instruments. Real software outcomes remain the ultimate correction mechanism.                                 |
| Independence                  | Vendors, sponsors, customers, and internal commercial incentives do not control research conclusions.                        |
| Minimum sufficient complexity | Complexity must earn measurable value in quality, knowledge, safety, economics, or reliability.                              |

# 6. How Powerfarm relates to change

Powerfarm operates as a laboratory, not as a technological belief system. Every recommendation may change. Every methodology may improve. Every architecture may be replaced. A technology remains only while it remains a good answer to a decision that matters.

Obsolescence is not institutional failure when the world has improved. A recommendation becoming obsolete is useful evidence about technological progress.

# 7. What Powerfarm is and is not

| **Powerfarm is**                                                                                        | **Powerfarm is not**                                                           |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| An experimental institution for applied intelligence in software production.                            | A foundation-model laboratory.                                                 |
| A longitudinal evidence and decision-intelligence system.                                               | A company whose identity depends on one agent, model, framework, or benchmark. |
| A producer of useful research, benchmarks, reports, decision models, and related intelligence products. | A leaderboard, trend blog, tool directory, or opinion-only consultancy.        |
| A user and integrator of frontier technology when it becomes decision-relevant.                         | A builder of proprietary infrastructure by default.                            |

# 8. Product philosophy

Powerfarm creates value by turning accumulated evidence into better decisions. Public work should contain genuine intellectual value. Paid work should earn its price through greater depth, structure, history, decision support, proprietary accumulation, or customer-specific application, not by hiding the basic method.

Powerfarm may eventually create software or data products built on its evidence base, but it must not anticipate a platform merely because a platform is imaginable. Products should emerge from demonstrated evidence, repeated use, and real demand.

# 9. The canonical system

Powerfarm keeps institutional truth deliberately small. The active canon contains five documents:

1.  PF-01 Powerfarm Charter: identity and permanent commitments.

2.  PF-02 Research and Evidence Standard: how Powerfarm learns and makes defensible claims.

3.  PF-03 Powerfarm Operating System: how Powerfarm decides, works, changes, and governs itself.

4.  PF-04 Intelligence and Technology System: the durable technical model for intelligence, software, evidence, and verification.

5.  PF-05 Products and Business System: how knowledge becomes products, customer value, publication, and revenue.

PF-06 Standard Documents Catalog is not a sixth source of institutional truth. It is the registry of document types and dormant future documents that may be instantiated if operational need appears.

> **One-home rule**
>
> Every important concept has one canonical home. Other documents reference that home instead of restating a competing version.

# 10. Change rule

The Charter is revisable, but it should change less often than methods, products, technology, or implementation. A Charter change is justified only when Powerfarm deliberately changes what it is, what it protects, or the permanent question it exists to answer.

Changes must preserve the previous version and state why the new text is better. Historical truth is not silently overwritten.

# 11. Founding principle

> **POWERFARM**
>
> We do not build the frontier. We learn how to use it better. Measure what exists. Learn what works. Change when reality changes.
