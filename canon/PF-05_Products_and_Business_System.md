Products and Business System

How Powerfarm turns evidence and operational knowledge into public value, paid products, and durable revenue

# 1. Business thesis

Powerfarm does not need to own the technologies transforming software production. Its commercial advantage comes from knowing, with increasing depth and historical context, how to use available technology better under real constraints.

> **Business principle**
>
> Powerfarm sells calibrated decision intelligence and accumulated evidence. It does not sell certainty, vendor enthusiasm, or proprietary tooling for its own sake.

# 2. Customer value

Powerfarm creates value for people and organizations making consequential decisions about AI-assisted software production. The customer need may be broad or specific, but the value proposition is consistent: reduce the cost of discovering what works, what does not, under which conditions, and with what confidence.

- Current best practice for a defined software-engineering decision.

- Comparative evidence across models, tools, routes, architectures, or methods.

- Cost, quality, time, reliability, and human-effort trade-offs.

- Historical trend and evidence about how the frontier is moving.

- Customer-specific evaluation when general evidence is insufficient.

# 3. Product classes

| **Class**       | **Purpose**                                                                                                | **Typical examples**                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Public          | Create trust, scrutiny, shared vocabulary, and useful open knowledge.                                      | Selected research, methods, benchmark results, frontier notes, changelogs, selected datasets.                                      |
| General paid    | Package reusable accumulated intelligence for multiple customers.                                          | Premium reports, subscriptions, proprietary datasets, historical intelligence, decision-support products, reference architectures. |
| Individual paid | Answer a customer-specific decision with Powerfarm methods and evidence.                                   | Private benchmarks, architecture studies, provider/model selection, repository studies, bespoke decision models.                   |
| Internal        | Increase Powerfarm's own research, evidence, reliability, efficiency, publication, or decision capability. | Experiment systems, evidence tooling, internal dashboards, routing systems, research automation.                                   |

Visibility and product class are separate axes. A paid product can use a public method. A public benchmark can keep sequestered tasks private. An internal tool does not become a product merely because it is useful.

# 4. Product forms

Powerfarm MAY create product forms such as:

- Living State of Software Engineering research.

- Frontier architecture and software-production reports.

- Intelligence route, local-vs-cloud, context, harness, or verification studies.

- Cost-of-verified-software and multi-objective benchmark products.

- Longitudinal trend reports and historical intelligence.

- Customer-specific decision studies.

- Structured data, dashboards, or APIs only after the underlying evidence system and repeated demand justify them.

This list is illustrative, not a roadmap. A product exists when there is evidence of value and a reason to maintain it.

# 5. Public value and paid depth

Powerfarm SHOULD publish enough real value to be trusted. The public layer must not be marketing fluff whose only purpose is to push readers behind a paywall.

| **Public layer**                                                      | **Paid/private layer**                                                                          |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Useful conclusions and selected evidence.                             | Deeper evidence, full history, richer data, and structured comparisons.                         |
| Methodology, definitions, confidence language, and corrections.       | Decision models, proprietary accumulation, customer-specific application.                       |
| Selected benchmark results, changelogs, and reproducibility material. | Sequestered material, complete experimental corpus, decision-support tooling where appropriate. |
| Enough transparency to challenge the work.                            | Enough proprietary depth to fund continued research and compound advantage.                     |

# 6. Productization gate

A research artifact, internal tool, dataset, or repeated service becomes a maintained product only when enough of the following are true:

- A real external decision or problem recurs.

- Powerfarm has differentiated evidence, history, method, or operational knowledge that materially improves the outcome.

- The value is understandable without pretending the evidence is stronger than it is.

- Maintenance cost and frontier drift can be sustained.

- The product benefits from reuse rather than remaining a one-off deliverable.

- External commodity alternatives do not already solve the problem sufficiently well.

- Customer demand, usage, willingness to pay, or strategic distribution value is credible.

- Security, confidentiality, licensing, and data rights are compatible with the product form.

> **No forced productization**
>
> Powerfarm is allowed to produce excellent research that never becomes a software product. It is also allowed to retire products when external progress makes them unnecessary.

# 7. Individual paid research

Customer-specific research may adapt task selection, constraints, confidentiality, and deliverable format, but it MUST NOT quietly lower evidence standards or manufacture certainty to satisfy a customer preference.

- The decision question and scope are explicit.

- Customer-provided evidence and data remain subject to agreed confidentiality and usage rights.

- Client-specific evidence does not silently enter public datasets or products.

- Anonymized or aggregated learning is reused only when permission, privacy, and contractual boundaries allow it.

- Recommendations distinguish customer constraints from general Powerfarm claims.

# 8. Internal products

Internal software exists to improve research capacity, evidence quality, reproducibility, reliability, operational efficiency, publication, or decision capability. Internal usefulness alone does not imply an external product opportunity.

Before commercializing an internal system, Powerfarm SHOULD re-run the productization gate from the customer's perspective rather than assuming that internal technical sophistication is market value.

# 9. Vendor and sponsor relationships

Powerfarm MAY collaborate with providers, vendors, hardware companies, or sponsors. Such relationships can improve access and research quality, but they cannot control the conclusion.

- Material credits, hardware support, early access, sponsorship, or other conflicts are disclosed when relevant to the work.

- Vendors may review factual descriptions and recommend configurations.

- Negative results are not suppressed in exchange for access or commercial benefit.

- A sponsor cannot define success metrics after seeing results or veto a legitimate conclusion.

# 10. Pricing and economics

Powerfarm pricing is a business decision, not a canonical price list. The durable principles are:

- Price reflects decision value, depth, uniqueness, service level, and market context rather than compute cost alone.

- Powerfarm understands the full cost to produce and maintain a product, including research, compute, human effort, support, and freshness.

- Paid work should create enough contribution to fund continued evidence accumulation and improve future products.

- Revenue incentives MUST NOT alter research measurements, confidence, or the existence of contradictory evidence.

- A product whose maintenance cost grows faster than its decision value SHOULD be simplified, repriced, replaced, or retired.

# 11. Publication, correction, and supersession

Products that contain empirical claims inherit the evidence and confidence rules of PF-02. Material errors are corrected visibly. Material recommendation changes preserve the previous state and explain why the current state changed.

Paid access does not permit lower traceability. Confidentiality may limit what can be shown externally, but the internal evidence lineage remains.

# 12. Product roadmap rule

The roadmap follows repeated customer decisions and accumulated Powerfarm advantage, not the existence of an attractive category. Future product ideas belong in working documents or PF-06 until evidence and demand justify active development.

A future Powerfarm Intelligence API is therefore an option, not a promise. It should exist only when the underlying evidence, decision models, query structure, and repeated demand are mature enough to make an API more useful than reports or direct research.

# 13. Business health

Powerfarm SHOULD evaluate business products on more than revenue alone. Relevant dimensions MAY include recurring customer value, gross contribution, reuse, decision impact, research compounding, customer trust, maintenance burden, and the rate at which the product becomes stale.

# 14. What Powerfarm refuses to become

- Vendor marketing disguised as independent research.

- A leaderboard business that protects the leaderboard after it stops measuring what matters.

- An opinion consultancy that cannot trace recommendations to evidence.

- A proprietary tool company by inertia.

- A paywall that withholds all useful public knowledge while using "research" as marketing language.

# 15. Final rule

> **Product principle**
>
> Knowledge determines what Powerfarm can responsibly say. Customer value determines what becomes a product. Evidence remains the foundation of both.
